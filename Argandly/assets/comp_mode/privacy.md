# Privacy Policy - Argandly

**Effective: April 9, 2026**

## What we collect

This app collects **no personal data**. It only saves user-initiated screen captures as image files to a local folder.

## What we do with files

- Screen captures are saved locally to the **platform's default Downloads folder** or to a user-selected custom folder.
- Files are never uploaded, shared, or transmitted to any servers.
- No network access, analytics, or telemetry.
